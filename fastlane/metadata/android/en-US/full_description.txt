<b>Setotower Downloader</b> is a free and open source Android downloader for media you are authorized to save. Paste a supported link, inspect the available title, thumbnail, formats and qualities, then download directly to your device.

<b>Downloading</b>

- download video or audio from supported public sources
- choose the container, resolution and audio quality before starting
- preserve audio and video together when the source provides separate streams
- inspect Cineb movie and series pages with posters, episode names and HLS qualities
- inspect magnet links and choose the torrent files you want to save
- share links to Setotower Downloader from any app
- pause, resume, cancel and retry downloads
- receive progress notifications while downloads run in the background
- save files in the device's Setotower download folder

<b>Media tools</b>

- merge compatible audio and video streams into a playable MP4
- remux supported HLS VOD sources without keeping a server-side media copy
- preserve the source title and use safe filenames
- show actionable errors when a source is private, restricted, unavailable or unsupported

<b>Privacy and safety</b>

- downloads run on the device whenever the selected flow supports it
- the Setotower service coordinates short-lived inspection metadata and does not act as a permanent media cache
- private or login-restricted content is not bypassed
- no account login is required for the Setotower app
- third-party engine and license notices are included with the source and release artifacts

Setotower Downloader only supports content you have permission to download. Availability and formats depend on the source provider.
